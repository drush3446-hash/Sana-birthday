A special day · A special girl

Dear Sana,

Kabhi-kabhi kuch log bina kuch extra kiye bhi din ko beautiful bana dete hain. Tum unhi logon mein se ho. I hope tum kabhi bhoolo nahi ki tum kitni special ho, tumhari presence kitni precious hai, aur tum kitni saari khushiyon ki deserving ho.

Meri wish hai ki tumhara yeh naya year tumhare liye happiness, confidence, success aur beautiful surprises lekar aaye. Tumhare dreams bade hon, tumhari smile bright rahe, aur jab bhi life difficult lage, tumhe yaad rahe ki tum usse handle karne ki strength rakhti ho.

Aaj sirf birthday celebrate nahi karna — aaj us amazing person ko celebrate karna hai jo tum ho. Keep shining, keep smiling, aur hamesha apni real self rehna.

Once again, happiest birthday. May life be extra gentle and kind to you. 💗

With all my love ♡
